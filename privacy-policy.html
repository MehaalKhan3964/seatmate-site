Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026 · Seatmate — carpooling in Pakistan

Seatmate connects riders and drivers who want to share a ride in Pakistan. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's handled. We've kept it in plain language on purpose — if anything is unclear, email us at privacy@seatmate.com.pk.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Phone number Used to sign you in. We send a one-time passcode (OTP) to verify it's really you.
Live GPS location Used to match you with nearby rides and to show pickup/drop-off points and live progress only while a ride is active.
Ride history Your past trips (routes, times, who you rode with) so you can see them again and so we can keep the platform safe and accountable.
Emergency contact (optional) A name and phone number you choose to add, used only to alert that person if you ever use an in-app emergency/SOS feature.
Basic profile info (name) Shown to the person you're sharing a ride with, so they know who they're meeting.
About your emergency contact: that person did not sign up for Seatmate. We only ever use their details to notify them if you trigger an emergency alert — never for marketing, never shared elsewhere. You can add, change, or remove them any time from your profile.

2. How your phone number is verified

To send your one-time passcode, we use Twilio, which delivers the code by SMS or by WhatsApp (a Meta platform). If your code arrives via WhatsApp, Meta's infrastructure is used to deliver that single message — Meta does not gain access to your Seatmate account, your rides, or your location, and we do not use WhatsApp for anything beyond OTP delivery.

3. Live location — when it's used

Seatmate only accesses your precise location while you have an active ride — to help match you with a driver or rider nearby, to draw your route on the map, and (if you choose to add an emergency contact) to share your position during an SOS alert. We do not track your location in the background or when you're not actively using the app.

4. How we store your data

Your information is stored on our backend (Supabase), transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access is limited to what's needed to run the service. Ride history and profile data are kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, this data is removed — see our data deletion page for how.

5. Who we share data with

We don't sell your personal data. We share only what's needed to run the app, with:

  • Supabase — our backend database and server functions
  • Twilio — delivers OTP codes via SMS/WhatsApp
  • Google Maps — powers the map and route matching

We may also share information if legally required to — for example, in response to a lawful request from a transport or other competent authority in Pakistan.

6. Your choices

  • You can add, edit, or remove your emergency contact at any time.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, by emailing privacy@seatmate.com.pk.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time — see Data Deletion.

7. Children

Seatmate is for people 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and let you know inside the app.

9. Contact us

Questions or concerns about your privacy: privacy@seatmate.com.pk
