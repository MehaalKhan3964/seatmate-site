Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Seatmate — carpooling in Pakistan · Operated by ALMISHQUN (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED, Islamabad, Pakistan

Seatmate connects riders and drivers who want to share a ride in Pakistan. Fares are agreed and paid directly between rider and driver, in cash; Seatmate does not take payment for rides and does not collect card details. This page explains what we collect, why, who else receives it, how long we keep it and how to delete it. Questions: privacy@seatmate.com.pk.

2. Who else receives data

These companies process data on our behalf to run Seatmate:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in service, file storage and server functions. It also sends your email sign-in codes through its email delivery.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Cloud API): delivers your phone sign-in code as a WhatsApp message, and delivers the message to your emergency contact (section 4). Meta receives the recipient's phone number and the message text so it can deliver them. We do not use WhatsApp for anything else.
  • Resend: sends the verification email if you choose to verify your email address.
  • Google: Google Sign-In; Google Maps (the map in the app sends technical information to Google, including your device model and operating-system version, crash information, your IP address and an SDK identifier, and our servers send place, route and distance requests to Google); and Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations, which deliver push notifications and use an app-installation identifier and the app version.
  • Expo: delivers app updates and push notifications. When the app starts it asks Expo's update service whether a newer version exists, sending your platform, app version and a random client identifier; Expo can see your IP address. Our servers send each notification's text and your push token to Expo's push service, which passes it to Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Other Seatmate users see the parts of your profile and rides that are relevant to them: name, profile photo, rating, ride details and the vehicle on rides you join or post. We may share information if the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful request from a competent authority in Pakistan.

3. How long we keep data

Most of your data is kept while your account exists and is removed when you delete your account (section 5). Some data is removed automatically on a schedule that we have checked is running:

  • Chat messages are deleted 7 days after the ride ends.
  • A location-sharing link and its location data are deleted as soon as the sharing period ends (checked every 10 minutes).
  • The record used to send the WhatsApp message to your emergency contact is deleted 24 hours after it is sent.
  • Notification history and sign-in records are deleted after 90 days.

We do not yet apply automatic deletion periods to verification document images, support cases, safety reports, audit records, push tokens or ride history after an account is deleted. These are kept as described in section 5 for as long as they are needed for safety, disputes and legal duties, and we will update this page when we set fixed periods.

Sign-in codes: when you ask for a sign-in code, we store your phone number, a hashed (one-way scrambled) form of the code rather than the code itself, the time the code expires (10 minutes after it is sent), how many guesses were made and when it was used. A code stops working after 10 minutes or once it is used or replaced, but we do not yet delete these records automatically: they are kept until you delete your account, which removes the records for your phone number. Records for a number that has no Seatmate account are not deleted automatically either. The short-lived codes used to verify an email address are stored the same way, hashed, with the email address and your account ID, and are also removed when you delete your account.

We do not currently keep scheduled backups of the database, so deleted data does not reappear from a backup. If we add backups, deleted data may remain in a backup until it expires and we will state that period on this page.

4. Your emergency contact

You can add one person as your emergency contact. Seatmate does not ask that person for permission before contacting them; please add only someone who knows you are doing this.

  • If you switch on “Share my location with this contact during rides” (it is off unless you turn it on), Seatmate sends your contact one WhatsApp message through Meta when you first start sharing. It contains your name and a private link that shows your ride location while the ride is on.
  • The link stops working when the ride ends and the location is deleted shortly after. Sharing is best-effort and is not a safety guarantee. In an emergency call 15 (Police) or 1122 (Rescue).
  • We use your contact's details only for this purpose, never for marketing. You can change or remove the contact in the app.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete Account) or by following our data deletion page. Deleting removes your profile, your sign-in identity (including your email login record), your requests, messages, notifications, saved places and routes, vehicles, verification records, push tokens, location shares and SOS records, and the files you uploaded: your profile photo and verification document images. Rides you were driving that have not finished are cancelled and affected riders are notified.

Some records are kept after deletion because safety, disputes and legal duties need them:

  • reports and no-show records involving you, kept as a restricted copy with identifiers hashed while they are needed;
  • verification review history and administrative audit logs, kept against an internal ID without your name or phone number;
  • support cases and fare offers, and reliability and vehicle history without your identity;
  • a one-way hash of your account identifier if your account was banned;
  • a cancelled ride may keep its route and time with the driver's identity removed.

We have not yet set fixed periods for these records. See section 3 for backups.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and emergency contact in the app, and switch location and notification permissions off in your phone settings.
  • Block users and report problems in the app.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for corrections, by emailing privacy@seatmate.com.pk.
  • Delete your account and data at any time (section 5).

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Verification document images are in a private store that only staff with a specific permission can open, with a logged reason, and staff actions on accounts are logged. No system is perfectly secure.

8. Children

Seatmate is for people 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

If we make meaningful changes we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app.
