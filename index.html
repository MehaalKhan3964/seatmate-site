How it works

01

Sign in with your phone

Every account is tied to a phone number, confirmed with a one-time code.

02

Find or offer a ride

Drivers post a route, a time and the seats they have. Riders search and request a seat.

03

Ride with a boarding code

When the driver accepts, the rider gets a boarding code. The driver enters it to start the ride, and the fare is paid directly in cash.

For riders and for drivers

Riders

Search by area and time, see the driver, the car and the fare before you ask for a seat, and message the driver about the pickup.

Drivers

Post the trip you are already making, including a regular weekly commute, choose how many seats to offer and accept the requests you want.

One account

The same account can find rides on one day and offer them on another.

Safety and trust

Verified phone numbers

Sign-in needs a one-time code sent to your number, so each seat has a real person behind it.

Women-only rides

Women drivers can offer rides that only women can request.

Report a problem

You can report a ride or a person from inside the app, and write to us at any time.

Seatmate is a pilot service for Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Fares are shown in the app and paid in cash directly to the driver. Please read the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy before you use the app.
