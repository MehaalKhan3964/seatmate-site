06 What's in the app

Built for how Pakistan commutes.

The features below are in the Android app you can download today. What is coming next is shown separately further down.

Car pool and motorbike pool

Post and find rides on four wheels or two. A bike ride offers one pillion seat; a car ride offers the seats you choose.

Smart route matching

Results are scored by how well a ride fits your pickup and drop-off, and each one shows the arrival time.

Women-only rides

Women drivers can offer rides that only women can request, and riders can filter for them.

Recurring commutes

Drive the same route every week? Turn on a recurring ride instead of posting it again each day.

Boarding codes

Passengers get a code when a seat is confirmed. The driver enters it to start the ride.

Chat in the app

Message the driver or passenger about the pickup, with quick replies. Everyone on a ride can also join its group chat.

Profiles and ratings

See a driver's vehicle, ride history and rating before you ask for a seat, and rate the ride afterwards.

English and Urdu

Use the app in the language you are comfortable with, including right-to-left Urdu layouts.

10 Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Write to support@seatmate.com.pk.

Which cities does Seatmate work in?

Seatmate is built for Pakistan. Rides appear wherever drivers post them, so what you find depends on your route and time of day. If nothing matches yet, try nearby areas or a different time, and ask a friend who drives your route to post it.

How do I pay for a ride?

The driver sets a fare per seat and it is shown on the ride before you ask. You pay the driver directly, in cash, during the ride. Seatmate does not take payment in the app.

How do I get started?

Download the Android app and sign in with a one-time code. Then search for a route to find a seat, or tap Offer a Ride to post a trip. The same account does both.

What is the boarding code?

When a driver accepts your request you get a short code. You show it to the driver as you get in, and they enter it to start the ride. It confirms you are in the right car.

Can I offer a ride on my motorbike?

Yes. Choose Bike when you post a ride. A motorbike carries one passenger, so the app offers one seat.

What are women-only rides?

A woman driver can mark a ride as women-only. Only women can request a seat on it, and riders can filter the feed for these rides.

What if there is no ride on my route?

Try nearby pick-up areas or a different time. In the next app update you will also be able to post a ride request, so drivers who pass your route can see that you are looking for one.

Is my location shared?

Your location is used to match rides and to show progress while a ride is active. If you add an emergency contact, you can give that one person a private link to follow your ride; it stops working when the ride ends. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a problem or delete my account?

Report a ride or a person from inside the app, or write to support@seatmate.com.pk. To delete your account and data, see how to delete your data or write to privacy@seatmate.com.pk.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Seatmate is available for Android only for now.
