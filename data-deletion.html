Account & Data Deletion

Seatmate — carpooling in Pakistan · Operated by ALMISHQUN (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED

You can delete your Seatmate account and the personal data linked to it at any time. You do not need to sign in to this page to find out how.

Option 1: Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open the Seatmate app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

When you confirm, your account and linked records are removed straight away, and the files you uploaded are removed immediately after. If a money-related item is still pending, the app will tell you to resolve it first. If you are driving a ride that has not finished, it is cancelled and affected riders are notified.

Option 2: Email us

If you cannot open the app, email privacy@seatmate.com.pk from the email address (or mention the phone number) linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will check it is really you and process the deletion. We aim to complete email requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, email address, gender and sign-in identity (including your email login record)
  • Your profile photo and any driver verification document images you uploaded
  • Your emergency contact, location-sharing links and SOS records
  • Your vehicles, saved places and routes, requests, messages, notifications and push-notification tokens
  • Your driver verification records, reports and no-show records you are part of (see below for the restricted copy)

What may be kept after deletion

Some records are kept because safety, disputes and legal duties need them:

  • A restricted copy of reports and no-show records involving you, with identifiers hashed, while a report or investigation needs it
  • Verification review history and administrative audit logs, kept against an internal ID without your name or phone number
  • Support cases and fare offers, and reliability and vehicle history without your identity
  • A one-way hash of your account identifier, if your account was banned, so the ban cannot be avoided by signing up again
  • A cancelled ride may keep its route and time, with the driver's identity removed

We have not yet set fixed retention periods for these records; we keep them only as long as they are needed for these purposes and will state the periods in our Privacy Policy when they are set.

Our database provider may keep backups for a limited time, so deleted data can remain in a backup until it expires. We will state the backup period once we have confirmed it.
